About Little Steps

Little Steps is an independent software and mobile app studio focused on building accessible, reliable, and user-friendly digital products. Our portfolio includes educational dictionaries, language-learning tools, IELTS preparation, driving education, casual games, creative apps, and video utilities. We aim to improve our products continuously through thoughtful design, development, and user feedback.
